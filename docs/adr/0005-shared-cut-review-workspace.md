# Shared cut review with protected manual choices

Accepted in the November 11, 2026 review-workflow interview (Q2 and Q3). Story
and Draft retain their workflow responsibilities but open a shared Preview
workspace with selected-shot context, image/video inspection and a detailed
queue, so separate pages do not present conflicting media inventories. Pacing
rearrangement protects hand-picked takes and manual trims by default and presents
conflicts in a proposed arrangement instead of silently replacing those choices.

Implementation and the remaining interaction decisions are tracked in the
[review-workflow interview](../plans/2026-11-11-review-workflow-performance.md).
